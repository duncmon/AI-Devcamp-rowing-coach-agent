# Training Plans

Place your rowing season and block training plans in this directory.

The `rowing_coach` agent can read and reference these plans to align weekly workouts with your periodization phases.

### Files

- `season_plan_2026_2027.md`: High-level multi-year macro plan (Sept 2026 – Sept 2027).
- `block_1_manosque_indoor.md`: Meso-cycle plan for Block 1 (Sept 2026 – 1st Sunday Dec 2026, targeting the Regional Indoor Championships in Manosque).
- Add additional block files or athlete-specific notes here as needed.
