# Masters Rowing Season Plan (2026 – 2027)

**Goal:** Sustainable performance, cardiovascular capacity, and injury prevention for Masters rowers across the 2026-2027 racing season.

## Season Macro-Cycles

### Block 1: Autumn Aerobic Base & Indoor Power (Sept 2026 – Dec 2026)
- **Primary Objective:** Build low-lactate aerobic capacity (UT2/UT1), technical precision on ergometer and water, progressive threshold tolerance (AT).
- **Target Event:** Regional Indoor Championships in Manosque (First Sunday of December 2026).
- **Focus:** 80% UT2/UT1, 20% AT. Masters recovery rules enforced.

### Block 2: Winter Distance & Head Race / Long Distance Conditioning (Jan 2027 – March 2027)
- **Primary Objective:** Aerobic volume, strength endurance, steady-state long pieces, transition toward sprint prep.
- **Target Events:** Head races / long-distance regattas.

### Block 3: Spring Sprint Transition (April 2027 – May 2027)
- **Primary Objective:** Anaerobic threshold and transportation pace (TR), stroke rate increases (28–34 spm), crew coordination.
- **Target Events:** Spring regattas (1000m masters distance).

### Block 4: Summer Championship Racing & Taper (June 2027 – July 2027)
- **Primary Objective:** Peak sprint power, race-pace execution (AN/TR), taper and recovery management.
- **Target Events:** Masters National Championships / FISA Masters Regatta.

### Block 5: Active Recovery & Transition (August 2027 – September 2027)
- **Primary Objective:** Cross-training, unstructured light rowing, restorative work before the new season.
