# Block 1 Training Plan: Road to Manosque Indoor Championships

- **Duration:** 14 Weeks (September 1, 2026 – December 6, 2026)
- **Target Competition:** Regional Indoor Rowing Championships in Manosque (First Sunday in December 2026: December 6, 2026)
- **Events:** 2000m individual race (Concept2 ergometer) and optional 500m / team relay.
- **Athlete Group:** Masters Rowers & Coach.

## Periodization Sub-Phases

### Weeks 1–4 (September 2026): Foundation & Volume Re-entry [COMPLETED]
- **Goal:** Re-establish steady state base (UT2), low drag factor (115–125), posture and stroke length.
- **Key Sessions:** 2x30min UT2 @ 18–20 spm, 3x15min UT1 @ 20–22 spm.

### Weeks 5–8 (October 2026): Aerobic Capacity & Threshold Introduction [CURRENT PHASE]
- **Goal:** Maintain 75–80% aerobic base while introducing Aerobic Threshold (AT) intervals.
- **Key Sessions:**
  - UT2 Steady State: 3x20min or 1x60min @ 18–20 spm (2k split + 16–20s).
  - AT Step-intervals: 3x10min / 3min rest @ 22–24 spm (2k split + 6–8s).
  - Rate Pyramids: 4x2000m (r20, r22, r24, r22).
- **Guardrails:** Max 1 AT session every 48–72 hours. No maximal anaerobic sprints yet.

### Weeks 9–11 (November 2026): Specific Power & Transportation (TR) Intervals
- **Goal:** Race pace habituation, lactate tolerance, transportation intervals.
- **Key Sessions:**
  - 4x1000m / 4min rest @ 2k goal pace (28–30 spm).
  - 5x500m / 3min rest @ 2k goal pace - 1s (30–32 spm).
  - UT2 volume maintained for recovery.

### Weeks 12–13 (Late Nov – Early Dec 2026): Taper & Race Prep
- **Goal:** Volume reduction by 40–50%, maintain intensity/sharpness, elevate Form (TSB > +10).
- **Key Sessions:** 2x1000m @ 2k pace, race starts (5-10-15 high strokes).

### Race Week (Week 14):
- Light openers, mental prep, weigh-in (if lightweight), race day on Dec 6, 2026.
